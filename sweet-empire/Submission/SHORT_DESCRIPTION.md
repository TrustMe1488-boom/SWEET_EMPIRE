# Short description — under 300 characters

Help Mia grow a bakery: deliver fresh treats, serve customers, expand your equipment and use Sophie's demand forecasts to plan ahead.

# Description — under 100 words

SWEET EMPIRE is a 3D bakery management game where you guide Mia from a small cookie operation toward a thriving business. Carry products to the checkout, keep customers supplied and invest in equipment and cooks. Follow a three-chapter campaign, prepare for Rush Hour and consult Sophie's demand forecasts as you grow. The interface is in Russian.

# AI integration — under 100 words

Customer profiles drive weighted, probabilistic product choices and patience. A separately trained Ridge regression model estimates demand for each product over the next 120 seconds using recent orders and bakery context. GDScript evaluates the model locally, with a rule-based fallback. Sophie presents the forecast and may suggest preparing more stock; the player decides what to deliver. Training data is synthetic. Interactive verification in the exported build remains required.

# Five key features

1. Carry products and serve a customer queue.
2. Buy and upgrade equipment, hire cooks and expand the bakery.
3. Complete campaign objectives, quests and Rush Hour.
4. Respond to customer preferences and demand forecasts.
5. Personalise Mia and the bakery with cosmetic items.
