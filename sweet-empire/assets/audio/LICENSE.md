# Audio sources

All WAV files in this folder are original procedural compositions and synthesized effects generated by Tools/Audio/generate_audio.py for SWEET EMPIRE. No recordings or music from other games were used. The project may use, modify and distribute these generated assets without third-party sample attribution.

Scores: 16-bar deterministic café arpeggios in three tempos; synthesized bell/pluck voices. Effects and street ambience are synthesized with deterministic oscillator/noise generation. Regenerate with `python Tools/Audio/generate_audio.py`.
